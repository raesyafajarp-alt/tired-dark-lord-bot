A small Node program that writes one 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, no network, no dependencies.

## What it writes

One monologue, exactly 100 words and 555 characters, original and written for this competition:

> Forty years ago I took this throne with a poisoned quill and a smile. The Sun-King bled gold on the marble; I signed the ledgers while the priests were screaming. Now the empire runs on paper. Petitions at dawn. Ravens at noon. The dead file their complaints in triplicate, and I stamp them. Denied. Denied. Denied. My generals want a war. My sorcerers want a sacrifice. Steward wants my signature on a form about forms. So let the hero come. Let him climb the black stairs, sword raised, destiny shining. I have left the door unlocked. And the kettle on.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
